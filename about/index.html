About Elaine Dinwiddie, LPC

Learn more about me

My name is Elaine Dinwiddie.

I have the training to help veterans and military personnel.

I am an Adult and Couples therapist specializing in helping people who would love to be set free from psychological stress, relationship issues, and the pressures of life.

Why therapy? 

Because I spend my working life helping people achieve meaningful, significant change, and positive core healing.

So I practice one-on-one and group, telehealth counseling in order to feature the successful progress of people like you toward personal growth in each of their unique journeys.

My Mission Statement

Interacting with adults and the elderly is a satisfying endeavor for me. I find gratification in working with these populations to assist them with behavioral and emotional problems, plan viable treatments, and conduct and interpret assessments. I am an inviting individual who enjoys listening to people tell their stories. As our journey on earth involves intrapersonal and interpersonal difficulties and unexpected life events, one needs a fellow traveler to assist them. I enjoy being that fellow traveler.

I am an LPC who helps clients with anxiety, depression, PTSD, bipolar disorder, phobias, and so forth with evidence-based counseling techniques and therapies. I have a theoretical and therapeutic lens based on trauma-informed care. I am trained in EMDR Level 2, EFT Professional, Gottman Couples therapy, CBT, DBT, CPT, ET, Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP I), Grief Therapy, Somatic Therapy, Attachment Therapy, Military and Veterans Therapy, and Women's Trauma Group.

My Story

Arriving from within a framework that is based on the understanding of how trauma affects people’s lives, I can help with depression, anxiety, bipolar, stress management, ADHD, PTSD, phobias, Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs), trauma, and problematic relationships.

Having a background in the Trauma-Informed Care (TIC) framework, I work with people who want to live happier and healthier lives.

The TIC is an approach to be applied where an individual has a history of trauma.

I have received a bachelor’s degree in Psychology, a master’s in Human Services: Marriage and Family, and a master’s in Professional Counseling; from Liberty University, in Lynchburg, VA.

I am an EMDR II Practitioner, Gottman Method Couples Therapy Level II, and a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP).

Professional Credentials & Specializations

Gottman Method Training & Certification

As a Level 2 Gottman Method Couples Therapist, I have completed advanced training in this evidence-based approach to couples therapy. This certification has equipped me with specialized skills to help couples build stronger relationships through:

  • Assessment techniques to identify relationship strengths and areas for growth
  • Methods to help couples manage conflict more effectively
  • Strategies to build friendship, intimacy, and shared meaning in relationships
  • Tools to help couples overcome the "Four Horsemen" of criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and stonewalling

EMDR & Trauma Qualifications

My EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) Level II training provides me with advanced therapeutic tools to help clients process traumatic memories and experiences. As a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP), I offer:

  • Specialized trauma-informed care using the latest research-based approaches
  • EMDR therapy for processing traumatic memories and reducing their emotional impact
  • Skills to identify and address trauma symptoms that may manifest as anxiety, depression, or relationship difficulties
  • Expertise in treating both single-incident trauma and complex developmental trauma

Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT) Certification

As an Emotional Freedom Technique professional, I utilize this powerful energy psychology approach to help clients achieve rapid relief from emotional and physical distress. EFT combines elements of cognitive therapy with acupressure techniques to help:

  • Process and release traumatic memories and emotional blockages
  • Reduce emotional and physical pain symptoms
  • Address cravings and addictive behaviors
  • Alleviate anxiety, phobias, and PTSD symptoms
  • Improve emotional regulation and stress management

Experience & Areas of Specialization

With over 15 years of clinical experience, I specialize in working with adults and couples facing:

  • Relationship challenges and communication difficulties
  • Trauma recovery and PTSD, including military-related trauma
  • Anxiety disorders and depression
  • Life transitions and identity issues
  • Grief and loss

I regularly participate in continuing education to stay current with the latest research and therapeutic approaches, ensuring my clients receive the most effective care available.

Common questions

What are Elaine’s qualifications and experience?

Elaine Dinwiddie is a Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC) in Oregon with a Master’s degree from Liberty University. She holds certifications in Gottman Method Couples Therapy Level 2, EMDR Level 2, and is a Certified Clinical Trauma Professional (CCTP I). She has extensive experience treating depression, anxiety, trauma, and relationship issues.

What therapeutic approaches does Elaine use?

Elaine uses evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR for trauma, Gottman Method for couples, Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), mindfulness-based therapy, and Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT). She specializes in trauma-informed care and military trauma therapy.

Does Elaine work with military personnel and veterans?

Yes, Elaine has specialized training and experience working with military personnel, veterans, and their families. She understands military culture and the unique challenges of military service, including PTSD, combat trauma, and deployment-related issues.

What insurance plans does Elaine accept?

Elaine accepts CIGNA, Moda, CHA, TriWest, Atrio, and other major insurance plans. She also works with military insurance (TRICARE) and can help you navigate veterans’ benefits. Please contact us to verify your specific insurance coverage.

Does Elaine offer telehealth sessions?

Yes, Elaine offers secure telehealth sessions for clients who prefer virtual therapy or are unable to attend in-person sessions. Her telehealth platform is HIPAA-compliant and easy to use, making therapy accessible regardless of your location in Oregon.

You can schedule an appointment by calling (541) 363-8817 or emailing elaine@adultstherapy.com. Elaine offers a free 15-minute phone consultation to discuss your needs, answer questions, and determine if she’s the right therapist for you.
