Contact the practice

The quickest way to reach Elaine is by phone. A complimentary 15-minute phone consultation is the usual first step: it is a chance to describe what brings you in and to decide together whether this practice is the right fit, with no obligation either way.

541-363-8817 elaine@adultstherapy.com

Hours

Tuesday through Friday, 11:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Pacific. Calls outside those hours are returned on the next working day.

Sessions

  • Individual therapy for adults runs 50 minutes.
  • Relationship and couples counseling runs 60 minutes.
  • Sessions are held by secure video, wherever you are in Oregon.

Insurance

Cascade Health Alliance (CHA), Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Atrio, Aetna, Moda, Providence, Regence, Cigna, TriWest, and Evernorth are accepted. If your plan is not listed, ask during the consultation call.

Where the practice works

The practice is based in Klamath Falls and works entirely by telehealth, so it reaches anyone in Oregon — Altamont, Chiloquin, Merrill, Bonanza, Malin, Sprague River, Beatty, Bly, and Rocky Point included. There is no office to travel to.

Before you call

You do not need a diagnosis, a referral, or an explanation prepared in advance. If you are not sure which approach fits, the therapy approaches page describes each one, and the consultation call is there to work that out together.

If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, do not wait for a callback. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, or call 911.
