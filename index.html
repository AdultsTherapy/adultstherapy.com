Relief from depression and anxiety

I work with adults living with trauma, depression, anxiety, OCD, and PTSD. Some people start out knowing exactly what they want to work on. Others only know that something is wrong and they are tired of carrying it alone. Both are a good enough reason to call.

Therapy is not a matter of being told to cheer up or try harder. It is steady work, at your pace, on the things that are actually in the way — and resilience, the capacity to come through hard things, is something that can be built rather than something you either have or do not.

What we might work on

  • Understanding what is happening, so it feels less like something wrong with you
  • Practical skills for getting through the hardest moments, including DBT distress tolerance
  • Processing traumatic experiences at a pace that stays safe
  • What you already have to draw on, which is usually more than it feels like

Thinking about starting

What brings people in

Depression or anxiety that has stopped being occasional. A relationship that has the same argument every week. Something that happened years ago and still decides how today goes. Feeling stuck, and not knowing what the next step would even be.

What the first session is

A conversation. You say as much or as little as you want to. We work out together what you are hoping will be different, and whether I am the right person to help with it. There is no commitment to a course of anything.

If now is not the time

That is a legitimate answer. Therapy works better when you have chosen it rather than been pushed into it, and the door stays open.

If you cannot wait, you do not have to.

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is answered 24 hours a day by someone trained to help, whether or not you are in danger right now. You do not need to be a client of mine, or of anyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I start therapy?

You can start therapy right away! I offer same-week appointments and flexible scheduling to fit your needs. Most clients begin within 3-5 days of their first contact.

Yes! I accept Cascade Health Alliance (CHA), Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Atrio, Aetna, Moda, Providence, Regence, Cigna, TriWest, and Evernorth insurance plans to make therapy accessible and affordable.

Common questions

What types of therapy do you offer?

We offer evidence-based therapies including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), EMDR for trauma, Gottman Method for couples, Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), mindfulness-based therapy, and Emotional Freedom Technique (EFT). We specialize in treating depression, anxiety, trauma, and relationship issues.

How much do therapy sessions cost?

Individual therapy sessions start at $150 for 50 minutes, and couples therapy sessions are $160 for 60 minutes. We offer a free 15-minute phone consultation to discuss your needs and determine if we’re a good fit.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. Cascade Health Alliance (CHA), Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Atrio, Aetna, Moda, Providence, Regence, Cigna, TriWest, and Evernorth are accepted, as well as cash, credit cards, and debit cards. If your plan is not listed, ask during the consultation call.

Do you offer telehealth sessions?

Yes, we offer secure telehealth sessions for clients who prefer virtual therapy or are unable to attend in-person sessions. Our telehealth platform is HIPAA-compliant and easy to use.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling us at (541) 363-8817 or emailing elaine@adultstherapy.com. We’ll start with a free 15-minute consultation to discuss your needs and answer any questions you may have.

What should I expect in my first session?

Your first session will be an initial consultation where we’ll discuss your concerns, goals for therapy, and develop a treatment plan. We’ll also review policies, answer questions, and ensure we’re a good fit for working together.
