Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I start therapy?

You can start therapy right away! I offer same-week appointments and flexible scheduling to fit your needs. Most clients begin within 3-5 days of their first contact.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes! I accept Cascade Health Alliance (CHA), Oregon Health Plan (OHP), Atrio, Aetna, Moda, Providence, Regence, Cigna, TriWest, and Evernorth insurance plans to make therapy accessible and affordable.

Can I do therapy online?

Absolutely! I offer telehealth sessions throughout Oregon, so you can get the help you need from the comfort of your home.

What if I'm not sure therapy is right for me?

That's completely normal! I offer a free consultation to help you decide if therapy is the right choice for you right now.
